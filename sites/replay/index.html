A seat at a park that doesn’t exist yet.

This is an independent fan reconstruction of the AECOM Railyards concept. The venue comes from our Blender model; the play, generic players, camera paths and splash are authored illustrations.

The river distance is measured horizontally from home plate in that model. The flight arc clears its static structures, but it is not an aerodynamic prediction or an official future field dimension.

Every selectable seat comes from the saved geometry. Architecture, clearances and sightlines remain inferred. Some seats and the boat have genuinely obstructed views; the ball marker is enlarged so you can follow the action. The optional locator also shows its position through obstructions.

Inspired by the shared-timeline approach in The Garden’s OG Game 4 replay. No player, venue, audio or animation assets were copied.
