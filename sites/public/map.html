Getting there

By L

Roosevelt (Red, Orange, Green) and Clinton (Blue) are each about a 15 to 20 minute walk. Nobody has proposed a closer station.

By Metra

Union Station is about a mile north, and it is the nearest stop.

By car

No parking is shown in the renderings. Canal Edge says it will be worked out later.

By water

Water taxis appear in the renderings, without an operator or a dock behind them yet.
