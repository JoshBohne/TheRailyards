    1. What moves where

      The story so far, in order. Scroll.

      ↓
    2. February 2024Earlier plan

      The Sox pitch The 78

      Related Midwest and the White Sox propose a ballpark on the 62 acres across the river, with a $1.2 billion public ask. It stalls, and the Chicago Fire take the site.

      MLB.com · Feb 2024
    3. March 3, 2026Under construction

      McDonald’s Park breaks ground

      The Fire’s privately funded 22,000-seat stadium starts rising at The 78. Target opening: 2028. The ballpark would face it across the river.

      Chicago Fire · Mar 3, 2026
    4. August 14, 2026Existing

      Amtrak wins the money to move

      Federal grants of up to $572 million for a new maintenance yard, plus $125 million from Justin Ishbia’s Canal Edge. This 47-acre yard south of Roosevelt is what would be freed up.

      Amtrak · Aug 14, 2026
    5. Fall 2026 → 2028Proposed

      The new yard goes to Bridgeport

      Look at the satellite: the rail yard is already there. Union Pacific’s Canal Street yard has sat beside Rate Field for over a century and idle since 2019. Amtrak would rebuild the tracks and add a shop building between 33rd and 35th. No new rail corridor, no new footprint.

      Block Club · Sept 9, 2026
    6. September 5, 2026Proposed

      The Railyards is unveiled

      Canal Edge shows AECOM renderings of a brick riverfront ballpark facing McDonald’s Park. The White Sox begin a multi-month study.

      Block Club · Sept 6, 2026
    7. September 8, 2026Opposition

      Bridgeport pushes back

      Residents and Ald. Nicole Lee rally against a 24-hour yard 100 feet from homes. Amtrak’s answer: passenger trains, not freight; fewer diesel switching moves than the old yard; LED lighting; less traffic off-peak. Without this yard, the ballpark has no site.

      WBEZ · Sept 8, 2026
    8. Through 2029Existing

      The Sox stay at Rate Field

      The lease and the state’s remaining stadium bonds both end after the 2029 season. Nothing about the ballpark, its financing or an opening date has been set.

      Sun-Times · Feb 8, 2024
