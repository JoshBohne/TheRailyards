Getting there

By L

The nearest stations are Roosevelt (Red, Orange, Green) across the Roosevelt Road bridge and Clinton (Blue) near Union Station, each roughly a 15 to 20 minute walk. No new CTA station or line has been proposed for the site. A Red Line infill station at The 78 was in that project’s original plan and remains unconfirmed.

By Metra

Union Station is about a mile north. The renderings mention “a nearby Metra station” without naming one, and Metra has not announced a stop at the site.

By car

No parking is shown in the renderings; Canal Edge says it will be worked out later. Existing downtown garages are about a mile away. Rate Field’s lots stay where they are.

By water

Water taxis appear in the renderings. No operator, route or dock has been announced.
