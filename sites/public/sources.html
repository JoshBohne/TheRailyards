The Railyards proposal

Canal Edge, led by White Sox minority owner Justin Ishbia, unveiled the concept on September 5, 2026. The renderings are by AECOM and the site is Amtrak’s 14th Street coach yard.

    Ald. Nicole Lee, Commissioner John Daley and mayoral candidates Mike Quigley and Susana Mendoza rally against the plan. The White Sox say they were “blindsided” by the pairing of the ballpark with the Amtrak facility.

  2. Sept 8, 2026
    White Sox owner-in-waiting reveals pictures of what proposed “Railyards” stadium probably won’t look likeField of Schemes · Neil deMause

    Notes that no architect has been chosen, no purchase price for the 47-acre site has been disclosed, no funding breakdown exists, and no parking is shown; a Canal Edge spokesperson said parking “will be ironed out.”

  3. Sept 8, 2026

    Real-estate trade coverage of the site plan and the land assembly.

Flags that the nearest L stations are a 15 to 20 minute walk. The land-cost figure it cites is uncorroborated elsewhere.

Amtrak’s move to Bridgeport

The ballpark depends on Amtrak vacating its yard. Amtrak plans a new maintenance facility on Union Pacific’s Canal Street yard beside Rate Field, funded by federal grants and Canal Edge.

  1. Sept 9, 2026
    What Amtrak has told Bridgeport about the Canal Street Yard, and what it hasn’tChicago City Wireunverified

    Sets out what Amtrak has disclosed about the Canal Street yard plan against the open questions residents raise. The site blocks automated reading; summary from the headline and excerpts.

  2. Sept 9, 2026
    Where Amtrak fixes its trains: how the Bridgeport yard compares to the other sixChicago City Wireunverified

    Compares the proposed Bridgeport facility with Amtrak’s six other maintenance yards. Not read in full.

  3. Sept 9, 2026
    Amtrak’s Bridgeport plan a “knife through the heart” of neighborhood, residents say at protestBlock Club Chicago · Kade Heather

    Site runs from Cermak Road to Pershing Road, about a block wide, roughly 100 feet from homes; trailer parking and storage to the north, trainshed and tracks to the south. Amtrak is using a federal categorical exclusion rather than an environmental impact study. Rep. Theresa Mah says lawmakers will write to the Amtrak board; Rep. Mike Quigley says Amtrak needs a facility, “but not here.”

  4. Sept 4, 2026
    Amtrak’s planned maintenance facility next to Rate Field faces local resistanceSox Machine

    Explains the NEPA categorical exclusion and notes a Bridgeport Environmentalists petition approaching 2,500 signatures.

  5. Sept 3, 2026
    Bridgeport, Chinatown residents push back on proposed Amtrak maintenance facility as railroad shares new detailsABC7 Chicago

    Television recap of the protest and Amtrak’s updated description of the facility.

  6. Sept 3, 2026
    Chinatown, Bridgeport residents sound alarm over proposed Amtrak facilityBlock Club Chicago

    Opposition spreads to Chinatown; residents question the lack of an environmental study.

  7. Aug 27, 2026
    Amtrak’s proposal to relocate maintenance facility to Bridgeport met with major pushbackNBC Chicago

    From the August 26 community meeting: South Canal Street between 33rd and 35th; an eight-track yard to the north and a four-story maintenance building to the south. This report says construction begins in 2027 and finishes in 2028, which conflicts with Amtrak’s fall 2026 start.

  8. Aug 21, 2026
    Bridgeport residents furious about Amtrak rail yard plans, and fear losing the White SoxBlock Club Chicago

    Describes about 60 acres of elevated Union Pacific land along Canal Street between 33rd and 35th. Sens. Durbin and Duckworth request more information; residents weigh legal action.

  9. Aug 15, 2026
    Feds’ $696M railway grant opens up Justin Ishbia’s South Loop megadevelopment planThe Real Deal

    Explains the land-swap mechanics. Its combined federal figure differs from Amtrak’s own $572 million plus $87 million breakdown.

  10. Aug 15, 2026
    Amtrak to relocate its Chicago maintenance facility from the South Loop to BridgeportAmtrak Unlimited forum

    Railfan discussion that aggregates operational details. Not a primary source.

  11. Aug 14, 2026
    Amtrak’s funding boost paves way for White Sox chairman-in-waiting Justin Ishbia’s South Loop visionChicago Sun-Times

    The 47-acre yard between Roosevelt Road and 18th Street is under contract to Shore Capital Partners. Ishbia: Canal Edge is “proud to partner with Amtrak and take responsibility for delivering this critical investment.” Part of $5.3 billion in federal rail funding to Illinois.

  12. Aug 14, 2026
    Amtrak receives federal grants for new Midwest maintenance facility and improvements to viaductsAmtrak Mediaprimary

    Up to $572 million for the maintenance facility and up to $87 million for viaduct repairs between Cermak and Pershing. Total project about $900 million including at least $125 million of private capital from Canal Edge. Construction to start fall 2026 and take about 18 months; roughly 3,000 construction jobs; supports 17 routes.

  13. 2026
    If Bridgeport stops the Amtrak facility, the rail yard doesn’t go away. It goes back to Union Pacific.Chicago City Wireunverified

    Argues the yard has been railroad property since the 1880s, that Union Pacific owns it, and that freight railroads have broad federal preemption over their own yards. The page blocked automated reading; the summary is from search excerpts only.

  14. 2026
    Durbin, Duckworth, Quigley urged feds to fund Amtrak’s Bridgeport rail yard in January; now they’re demanding answersChicago City Wireunverified

    Reports that the same members of Congress who sought the funding in January 2026 are now pressing Amtrak for details. Not read in full.

What is still unknown

As of September 9, 2026, no source answers these. The site will update as reporting does.

  1. Site
    Purchase price and acreage.

    Reports give 47, 48 and “over 80” acres and no dollar figure for the Amtrak parcel.

  2. Money
    Public funding for the ballpark.

    Canal Edge describes the stadium as privately financed, but no infrastructure or subsidy request has been filed.

  3. Schedule
    Amtrak’s start date.

    Amtrak says fall 2026; NBC Chicago reported 2027. No stadium construction or opening date exists.

  4. Review
    Environmental review.

    Whether the Federal Railroad Administration has granted the categorical exclusion, and on what date, is not confirmed in a primary document.

  5. Access
    Transit, parking and the water taxi.

    No new station, garage, dock or operator has been proposed by any agency.

  6. Design
    The architect.

    AECOM produced the concept images; Canal Edge says the architect has not been chosen.
