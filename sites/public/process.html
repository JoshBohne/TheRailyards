Why “API-equivalent”? The work ran through subscriptions and local agent tools. The estimate applies public list pricing to the logged usage; it is not a claim that $102.78 was charged.

Why can model time exceed wall time? Luna’s six workers ran concurrently. Adding their response durations produces more model time than elapsed clock time.

Why so many cached tokens? Long agent sessions repeatedly read the repository, scene specifications, and prior context. Cache reads describe processing, not newly authored output.
