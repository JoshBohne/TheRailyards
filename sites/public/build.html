I’m not a 3D modeler.

I'm a software engineer who wanted to test GPT-6 Astra and Fable 5.1 to see how closely they could recreate the renderings in Blender. This is a fan-made exploration, not an architectural proposal. Unseen spaces, structural details, dimensions, and sightlines rely heavily on interpretation, so some elements may be inaccurate, inconsistent, or impractical to build.

- Josh Bohne
