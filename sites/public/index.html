What this study can and cannot tell you

It can illustrate relative scale, modeled distances, overlapping sightlines, and possible experiences. It cannot establish final architecture, access arrangements, seat quality, safety, or what will be approved and built.

The arrival terrace, gatehouse, restaurant connection, and public park grades remain inferred. The river movie uses an enlarged marker and a chosen arc; it does not simulate air resistance, spin, or wind.
