## Search boundary and negative findings

- Checked the September 5 announcement coverage and public media embedded by Sox On 35th, Chicago Sun-Times, CBS Chicago, and FOX 32 Chicago. The Sox On 35th WordPress media endpoint for the release post reports exactly three attachments: south-day, north, and site plan.
- Sun-Times exposes the existing 5000×3333 south-day AECOM image and did not expose an additional proposal gallery.
- CBS exposes the two 5000×3333 AECOM files listed above. FOX 32 exposes three scaled AECOM copies plus the locator map.
- No official AECOM or Canal Edge asset gallery, interior view, or detailed drawing sheet was found in this bounded public search. The released A3 image is the bridge-facing evidence; B4 is the north aerial; B6 is the south-day aerial.
- Older **2024 The 78** proposal material was excluded from acquisition and interpretation.
