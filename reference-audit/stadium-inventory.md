# White Sox Railyards stadium reference inventory

This is a source-grounded discrepancy inventory for the September 2026 west-bank Railyards concept and the immediate stadium-facing public realm. It records what the three proposal views resolve, how the current v2 render compares, and the smallest geometric correction that would close each visible gap. It is an audit artifact; it does not edit the Blender scene or claim that inferred concept-art dimensions are surveyed facts.

## Method and evidence boundary

I compared the released AECOM views with the current v2 north, south, and bridge renders, using the existing registered scene traces and the cropped comparison set in [crops](./crops/). The linked crop previews retain the original derivative files used for the side-by-side inspection; the canonical coordinates below are measured in the supplied 5000×3333 CBS copies. Each migrated ROI also carries its prior derivative box as legacy_xywh in [stadium-elements.json](./stadium-elements.json), preserving the old measurement trail. The selected gallery controls are useful registration checks only: north RMS 2.9 px/max 5.6 px, south RMS 34.2 px/max 111.2 px at the left-field pavilion, and bridge RMS 8.2 px/max 22.7 px.

Confidence means: **high** = resolved in more than one view; **medium** = clear in one view or partly occluded; **inferred** = a concept-art interpretation or phase assumption. Relative heights, hidden sections, structural sizes, and future context remain inferred unless the source repeatedly resolves them.

## Top five fidelity risks

1. **Bowl / canopy silhouette and tier rhythm (STAD-001–004):** preserve the calibrated horseshoe and four measured deck bands; add articulation, dark edge depth, and ribs rather than globally scaling the bowl.
2. **River facade envelope (STAD-005–006, STAD-016):** restore the deep brick mass, tall lower arches, dark glazed upper fascia, and two-level river arcade.
3. **Clock tower and lantern (STAD-007–008):** retain the registered tower anchor while widening the masonry shaft and changing the lantern to glowing glazing with dark faces.
4. **Outfield banks, boards, and gantries (STAD-003, STAD-009–012, STAD-017–018):** add independent bleacher/terrace masses, structural board backs/supports, and steel floodlight frames.
5. **Stadium / bridge / river relationships (STAD-020–024):** make covered links land in real concourses, rebuild the foreground drawbridge silhouette, and layer the public river edge and entry lawn.

## Measured constraints to preserve

- Seating bands currently represented in the scene: t/z **0–0.34 / 14–24**, **0.40–0.49 / 27–30**, **0.55–0.65 / 33–36**, and **0.70–0.93 / 39–47**. Keep these four bands and their calibrated back curve; the mismatch is fascia, aisle, and end articulation.
- Canopy: retain the traced inner/outer silhouette, **13 canopy rows**, and the existing **3.1 m barrel rise** (the envelope notes also describe an inferred 3.25 m crown lift in the v3 pass; treat that difference as an implementation choice to validate against the trace, not a source measurement). Add fascia/truss/ribs as visible section.
- Field scale prior: a **27.432 m (90 ft) base square** and a broad **4–6 m warning track** are reconstruction choices anchored by the source; the source concept art does not constitute a survey.
- Tower: retain the calibrated roof anchor **[81.54, -76.38, 68]** until all three camera projections are rechecked.
