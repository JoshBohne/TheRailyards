# V3 delivery and review record

V3 preserves v1 and v2 and extends the reconstruction across all 28 catalogued source-element groups. The implementation ledger is [ELEMENT-REVIEW.md](ELEMENT-REVIEW.md); evidence and unresolved interpretations are in [SOURCE-AND-ASSUMPTIONS.md](SOURCE-AND-ASSUMPTIONS.md).

The priority sequence was stadium geometry and details, immediate district and river crossings, then the finite real skyline visible beyond the outfield. Bounded research and component workers supplied source inventories and isolated generators; one owner integrated the scene and reviewed the combined views.

## Completed work

- Rebuilt the field, outfield seating, envelope, tower details, light gantries and supported terrace shades; added native board graphics, fine glazing, furniture and activity.
- Added source-led medical and entertainment buildings, raised public park, lower promenade, Roosevelt structure, covered rail links and landings, southern rail bascules, future southern building masses, soccer venue and event staging.
- Added geographically placed skyline landmarks and mapped corridor buildings; retained per-source confidence and rejected unverified future-tower replacements.
- Preserved three calibrated exterior cameras, added four usable interior views, a site view and a southern rail detail camera.
- Rebuilt from an empty CLI process, reopened the deliverable through real Blender MCP and verified collection presence, finite geometry, field dimensions, cameras and missing dependencies.
- Reviewed native renders in the browser. Final corrections removed field z-fighting, cleared the third-base camera from an obstructing fascia, added the southern bascule hinge foundation and corrected its approach tracks.

## Review limits

This is a detailed conceptual reconstruction, not an assertion of photographic equivalence. The comparisons still show differences in surface texture, crowd character, landscape density, atmospheric color and some architectural proportions. Concealed structure and source-inconsistent geometry remain inferred. North and south covered rail links use explicitly named source variants; this conflict has not been solved by claiming a surveyed common alignment. Future tower envelopes and the distant city are less detailed than the stadium. Camera-control residuals measure only selected calibration points.

The gallery exposes original artwork, actual model renders, overlay controls, v2 comparison and full-size diagnostic views so those differences remain reviewable.
